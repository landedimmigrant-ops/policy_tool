# Federal Policy Pathways

An interactive walkthrough of how a decision moves through the Canadian federal government, and where a researcher can get in.

Six stages, each with two views:

- **The machine** — what happens, who is in the room, what it produces, how long it takes, and how you can tell you are at this stage.
- **Your opening** — how open the stage is to an outsider, routes ranked by leverage, what you need in hand, timing, the procedural rules with links, and the mistake people make.

Below the stages: a signal matcher for working out which stage your issue is at, and a dated list of live openings.

Every claim carries one of two markers:

- **Verified** — checked against the official source page on 3 September 2026.
- **Check** — inferred from pattern; confirm before relying on it.

## Scope

Federal layer only. Quebec and other provinces to follow.

## Status

v1, 3 September 2026. Dated items go stale in weeks; treat the "Live right now" list as a snapshot.

Built for Pathways to Impact, Concordia University.
